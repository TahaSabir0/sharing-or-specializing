# 22. Eval-surface composition — what each split actually measures (the board is a biased slice)

**Scope.** The per-split × per-task × per-dataset composition of our three eval surfaces, and the
**bias each one imposes on how we read our own performance.** Motivated 2026-08-03 when the ML donor
discussion surfaced that our *weakest* datasets are often *absent* from the Codabench board. This ledger
is the numbers-of-record for "does optimizing X reflect the real hidden test, or just the slice we can
see?" — the standing rule now codified in `CLAUDE.md` (Working notes) and
[[project-per-dataset-not-pooled-metric]]. Narrative + decision: `PROGRESS.md` 2026-08-03.

**Splits.** `true_test` (4,574) + `validation-public` (5,348) are carved from organizer `training/`
(leak-free frozen `internal_splits_v2`). **`validation-hidden` (1,959; 1,703 scored) = the Codabench
validation leaderboard** ("the board"). All counts verified 2026-08-03 by direct scan of the frozen
split jsonl + the organizer `validation-hidden/*_questions_val.json` (`ssh ws`).

## §2 — The three surfaces are largely DISJOINT (only true_test is representative)

- **Classification: board ∩ public = ∅.** Board = {dental, fundus, iugc, bone_marrow}; public =
  {CMMD, bcn20000, retino, neojaundice, BUSI, BUS-UCLM, endo}. **Zero overlap.**
- **Multi-label: board = periapical only; public = chestdr only.** Disjoint.
- **Regression: public has NONE; board = boneresorption only** (iugc regression only in true_test).
- **Counting + report-gen: absent from the board entirely** (0 rows).
- **⇒ `true_test` is the ONLY split that contains every task and (nearly) every dataset.** It is our sole
  breadth-faithful proxy for the real hidden *test* set. The board and validation-public are each a
  **narrow, non-representative sample** — and different samples from each other.

## §3 — Direction of bias: what the board over-/under-states (cross vs our per-dataset strength)

Our per-dataset true_test performance (deployed 2048² flagship; cls from `stats/16 §3a`, ML from
`§3c`, reg from `§0`):

| task | board dataset(s) | our strength there | ABSENT from board | our strength there | **board bias** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **cls** | dental 0.629→~0.75¹, fundus 0.672→~0.72¹, iugc >0.82, bone_marrow >0.82 | mid-to-strong | **CMMD 0.517 (chance), bcn20000 ~0.5–0.64, retino 0.82** | our **worst** cls sets | **FLATTERS** (excludes our disasters) |
| **ML** | periapical ~0.55 real / **0.64 scored**² | our **stronger** ML set | **chestdr ~0.46** | our **weaker** ML set | **FLATTERS** |
| **reg** | boneresorption ~19 MAE | our **weaker** reg set | iugc (r=0.96) | our **strong** reg set | **PENALIZES** |

¹ scorer *deflates* dental −0.121 / fundus −0.048 (option-letter family pooling, `stats/16 §2c`) — real ability higher.
² scorer *inflates* periapical +0.088 (compound comma label split, `stats/16 §2c`).

**Net: the board over-states our classification + multi-label and under-states our regression.**

**★ Consequence — the board sits at the OPTIMISTIC end of our cls range.** true_test 11-dataset macro
BA = **0.7484**; validation-public 7-dataset (CMMD/bcn20000-heavy) macro = **0.6399**; the board 4-dataset
subset scores ~**0.81**. So our "true" cls level is bracketed **[0.64 … 0.81]** and *which end the real
test lands on depends on its unknown composition* — the board is the rosy bound, precisely because it
omits CMMD + bcn20000 (which are **huge** in the organizer pool: 910 + 716 rows in public alone).

## §4 — Decision implications (the "real final placings" lens)

1. **Do not read the board's cls 0.81 as our cls level.** Our breadth-faithful estimate is true_test
   per-dataset macro ≈ **0.75**, and a real test that samples CMMD/bcn20000 pulls toward **0.64**.
2. **Two of our planned cls fixes give ZERO board feedback.** `stats/16 §4`'s **retino grading rubric**
   and **CMMD threshold** target datasets that are **validation-public only** — real-test value, no board
   signal. Conversely the board's dominant cls dataset (**dental, 53%**) is **artifact-capped** (−0.121),
   so the single biggest board lever has a low ceiling.
3. **Split cls effort by what it serves, and label it:** (a) *board-facing* = dental/fundus/iugc/bone_marrow
   (limited headroom; dental capped); (b) *true-test-facing* = CMMD/bcn20000 (our real weakness, but
   **capability-bound** — CMMD subtype is visually ill-posed, bcn20000 is unstructured perception).
   **There is no cheap board-validated cls win** — chasing #1's 0.87 is a grind on sets we're already OK at.
4. **ML donor screen re-scoped:** periapical (board, already our stronger set, inflated) has low headroom;
   the real ML gap is **chestdr** (~0.46), which is **capability-bound (more data, lower score) and invisible
   on the board.** Judge any ML arm on **both** datasets; never lift periapical while sinking chestdr.
5. **Regression stays closed — and is even safer than the board shows** (board = our weak boneresorption
   slice; a real test including iugc scores us *higher*).
6. **Standing method:** decide by **true_test per-dataset macro weighted by plausible real-test
   composition**, treat any board gain as *necessary-not-sufficient*, and state explicitly when a decision
   is board-slice-only.
