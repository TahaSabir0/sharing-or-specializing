# 11. Backbone Bake-off Stats — MedGemma 1.5 & Gemma 4 E4B vs Qwen3-VL (classification)

Head-to-head **classification** bake-off of two challenger backbones against the committed Qwen3-VL-8B,
launched 2026-07-06 on the workstation `ws`. Motivation: the organizers' `BASELINE.pdf` shows
**MedGemma 1.5 leading fine-tuned classification (pooled BA 0.697)** — the one baseline result pushing
against our settled Qwen3 backbone (see `PROGRESS.md` 2026-07-03 / 2026-07-06, [[project_medgemma-bakeoff]],
[[project_gemma4-exploration]]). Classification is ~50% of the ChallengeR ranking, so it's the decisive
metric to contest. This ledger records the matched, cross-validated result.

## Setup (identical across all three backbones — only model + template + lora_target vary)

- **Task:** classification specialist adapter, per-dataset **macro balanced accuracy** (the ChallengeR-faithful
  metric, [[project_per-dataset-not-pooled-metric]]) — NOT pooled BA. 10 cls datasets.
- **Data:** frozen `internal_splits_v2` A/B cls decks (25,911 rows: A=12,942 / B=12,969), k1 + `Image-i:`
  markers, endo excluded. Cross-validated **both directions**: train A → eval B (**AB**) and train B → eval A (**BA**),
  then **direction-average**. Seed 1337.
- **Recipe:** QLoRA r8, 3 epochs — the exact Qwen3 cls-specialist recipe (job 1505). Swap ONLY the base model +
  chat template (+ Gemma 4's explicit `lora_target`).
- **Envs:** Qwen3 = `flare-qwen3` (transformers 4.57.1 / torch 2.3.1). MedGemma 1.5 = `flare-gemma26`
  (torch bumped to 2.6.0+cu124 for the gemma3 attention-mask floor; transformers 4.57.1, LF v0.9.4).
  Gemma 4 E4B = `flare-gemma4-lf095` (transformers 5.6.0 / LF v0.9.5 / peft 0.18.1 / bnb 0.49.2 — a fresh
  isolated transformers-5 stack; Gemma 4 needs LF ≥ v0.9.5).
- **Fairness:** image budget 768×768 for all three (Gemma 4 pools to ≤280 soft tokens → no token advantage).
  All prompts train↔infer **byte-identity MATCH**. Gemma 4's empty reasoning-channel tag stripped in eval
  (same class as Qwen3's `</think>`; cls answers are bare labels → clean).
- Models: Qwen3-VL-8B (~8 B), MedGemma 1.5 (**4 B**, Gemma3 + SigLIP, medically pretrained), Gemma 4 E4B (~8 B,
  general). 0 malformed outputs on any run.

## Verdict — Qwen3-VL HOLDS the backbone (bake-off does NOT flip the decision)

MedGemma's cls win is **real but far too small to justify a switch**, because of the **single-model /
single-architecture constraint** (can't route cls→MedGemma + rest→Qwen3):
- cls is ~50% of ranking → +0.0074 cls ≈ **+0.0037 on the overall score**.
- Against that, Qwen3's advantages on the *other* tasks are much larger: organizer baseline detection
  **0.803 vs MedGemma 0.092** (Qwen3 also owns counting + report-gen). Qwen3's detection edge alone
  (~+0.7 F1) dwarfs the cls trade even at detection's smaller case weight.
- ⇒ Adopting MedGemma would surrender Qwen3's multi-task dominance to gain ~0.004 on classification. **Not worth it.**

**Paper value (high):** a clean, controlled, cross-validated result — the medically-pretrained backbone wins
classification narrowly *and on the interpretable rare-class sets*, while the general grounding-strong backbone
wins overall. Gemma 4 documents the "newer general architecture ≠ better on niche medical distributions" point.
See [[project_backbone-survey]].

## Why does MedGemma win cls? — frozen vision-encoder linear probe (2026-07-06)

Isolation experiment to test whether MedGemma's cls win is caused by its **medical vision encoder** (MedSigLIP = SigLIP-400M fine-tuned on ~33M medical images) vs Qwen3-VL's general SigLIP2-400M tower. Both are 27-layer/1152-d SigLIP cousins → a frozen-feature probe isolates the *pretraining* difference (no LM, no SFT confound). Setup: same images, mean-pooled pre-merger 1152-d patch tokens at ~448², identical standardized probe; per-dataset **dir-avg macro BA** (logistic = primary, MLP(256) = cross-check). Artifacts on ws `/share/sda/ugrip/taha/medsiglip_probe/` (`probe_results.json`, cached embeddings).

| dataset | grp | MedSigLIP (log) | Qwen3-ViT (log) | Δ log | Δ mlp |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| retino | WON | 0.749 | 0.672 | **+0.077** | +0.105 |
| bone_marrow | WON | 0.817 | 0.784 | +0.033 | +0.037 |
| fundus | WON | 0.921 | 0.889 | +0.032 | +0.050 |
| BUS-UCLM | WON | 0.856 | 0.845 | +0.012 | +0.017 |
| bcn20000 | WON | 0.434 | 0.424 | +0.010 | −0.009 |
| dental | ctrl | 0.498 | 0.424 | +0.074 | +0.091 |
| CMMD | ctrl | 0.529 | 0.485 | +0.044 | +0.055 |
| iugc | ctrl | 0.984 | 0.975 | +0.009 | +0.010 |
| **WON mean** | | | | **+0.033** | +0.040 |
| **CTRL mean** | | | | **+0.042** | +0.052 |

**Verdict — hypothesis PARTIALLY supported; do NOT escalate to grafting.** MedSigLIP has a real, consistent frozen-feature edge (wins 7/8 datasets) → medical pretraining does produce better medical visual representations. **BUT the edge is BROAD, not won-specific** (control mean +0.042 ≥ won mean +0.033): MedSigLIP is also better on CMMD (+0.044) and dental (+0.074) — datasets the *full MedGemma VLM LOST* to Qwen3. ⇒ **frozen-encoder quality does NOT predict VLM cls performance** (downstream LM+SFT flips it), so grafting MedSigLIP into Qwen3 (Route C′) is not guaranteed to pay off and does not clear the bar. Only retino (+0.077) is a clean won-specific case. Caveats: dental control labels noisy (jaw-ambiguous); fundus/CMMD reduced to a clean 1:1 subtask; mild resolution asymmetry (MedSigLIP ~1024 tok vs Qwen3 ~750) may inflate MedSigLIP's edge. **Path 1 (medical encoder) CLOSED as a paper-worthy isolation result;** the higher-EV lever is the resolution-mismatch fix ([[project_resolution-mismatch-bug]]).

## Resolution-mismatch fix — capped(768²) vs uncapped(native) inference, same harness (2026-07-06)

Existing §4a/§4c specialists, no retrain; only `--image_max_pixels` varies (0=native vs 589,824=768²-matched-to-training). Endo-excluded per-dataset macro. **The fix (capping) HELPS both tasks + runs ~4× faster (1.3 vs 5.5 s/case, 8.3 vs 12.5 GB) → SHIP IT.**

| task / dir | uncapped (status quo) | capped (fix) | Δ |
|---|---|---|---|
| classification A→B | 0.6545 | 0.6607 | +0.0062 |
| classification B→A | 0.6622 | 0.6648 | +0.0026 |
| **classification dir-avg** | 0.6584 | **0.6628** | **+0.0044** |
| multi-label A→B | 0.529 | 0.5419 | +0.013 |
| multi-label B→A | 0.524 | 0.5286 | +0.0046 |
| **multi-label dir-avg** | 0.5265 | **0.5353** | **+0.0088** |

Runtime: capped ~1.3 s/case & 8.3 GB vs uncapped ~5.5 s/case & 12.5 GB (**~4× faster**).

**Verdict — SHIP the free 768²-cap fix.** Net-positive on BOTH tasks, BOTH directions at the macro level (cls +0.0044, ML +0.0088), zero retrain, and ~4× faster inference. The accuracy gain is *small* but it's free and directionally reliable, and the speedup is large (helps the ≤1.5× runtime gate).

**Honest wrinkles (documented):**
- **Per-dataset effect is NOISY at dir-avg, not the clean "large-image → capping helps" story the A→B-only view suggested.** Capping *helps* bone_marrow (+0.050), retino (+0.033), dental (+0.016) but *hurts* CMMD (−0.015), bcn (−0.014), BUSI (−0.011), fundus (−0.010); signs flip between directions (e.g. bcn +0.016 A→B → −0.043 B→A). Only the **macro net** (+0.0044) is reliable; don't over-read per-dataset.
- **ML implication holds:** capping lifts Qwen3 ML to 0.5353, halving the iv3-ML gap (iv3 0.541; +0.006, was +0.012) → part of "InternVL wins multi-label" was our own resolution bug.
- **Harness drift resolved:** fresh uncapped cls dir-avg (0.6584) reproduces §4a (0.660) within ~0.002 (the earlier −0.009 on A→B was a per-direction artifact offset by +0.007 on B→A) → the comparison is faithful.

**Bake-off re-settlement (both models now train/infer-matched):** MedGemma 896²-matched **0.6674** vs capped Qwen3 768²-matched **0.6628** → **MedGemma still +0.0046 on cls — NARROWED from +0.0074, NOT flipped.** Fixing Qwen3's resolution handicap closed ~⅜ of the gap but didn't reverse it. The residual could be genuine or could close if Qwen3 is tested at a *higher* matched resolution (896²+/2048²) — that's the open high-res retrain experiment. **Qwen3 holds the backbone regardless** (the multi-task story is unchanged). See [[project_resolution-mismatch-bug]], [[project_medgemma-bakeoff]].
