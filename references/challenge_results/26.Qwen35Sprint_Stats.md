## §0 — Setup facts + gates (all PASS unless noted)

- **Template identity:** LF `qwen3_5` template + `enable_thinking:false` renders BYTE-IDENTICAL to the
  inference `apply_chat_template(..., enable_thinking=False)` path — verified on the box AND on ws, at
  2048² and 768², incl. the ML system column. Convention: empty `<think>\n\n</think>` sits in the
  **prompt** (no loss) — differs from the 8B convention (think-in-target with loss) but is
  train↔infer-consistent, which is the property that matters.
- **★ Three unset-vendor-default landmines found on this backbone, all pinned:** (1) thinking default ON →
  pin `enable_thinking=False`; (2) no generation_config → no sampling defaults to leak (good), but ALSO
  (3) **no EOS in generate() → the model answers correctly then babbles a fake `user` turn to the token
  budget** (caught in eval smoke 08-31; fix = pin `eos_token_id=[<|im_end|>, eos]` explicitly).
  Third instance of [[project-unset-defaults-pattern]].
- Speed/memory anchors: 2048² train bsz2/ga4 ≈ 21.5 s/it clean / ~24.5 contended, 44.4 GB peak (96 GB box);
  768² train bsz1/ga8 ≈ 9.7–12.3 s/it, ~11–18 GB peak (A5000); ~2.3× slower than the 8B per step at 2048²
  (64 layers vs 36 + immature linear-attn kernels). 4-bit inference ≈ 7.6–8.6 GB; ~0.9–1.5 s/case at 768².

## §1i — report-gen "normal-on-abnormal" under ONE definition, 8B vs 9B — DONE 2026-09-10 (CPU, saved predictions)

**Why this exists.** The sprint log / §4 row / paper brief carried "mode-collapse 76% → 14.5%". Those are two DIFFERENT
statistics: the 8B **75.9%** (`stats/16 §3f`) = *fraction of abnormal-gold sentences answered with a normal statement*
(computed 07-26 by an ad-hoc pass whose rule was never written down); the 9B **14.5%** = `reportgen_sanity_proxy.py`'s
`collapse_fraction` = *fraction of rows whose gold ≠ the single modal sentence but whose prediction IS that modal
sentence* ("mediastinal contours are within normal limits."). The 8B scores **14.7%** on that same statistic ⇒ the
"76 → 14.5" line was a definition swap, not a result. **Do not use it in the paper.** (Veronika caught this, 09-10.)

**Method (one rule, both models):** `stats/predictions/qwen35_sprint/reportgen_normal_on_abnormal.py`. A sentence is a
