### §2.1 Resolution lever is EXHAUSTED on 3 of our 4 weakest cls datasets
CMMD 0.977 · dental **1.000** · bcn20000 **1.000** · fundus 0.968 — at 2048² these receive
essentially every pixel they have. Their deficits are **not** pixel-supply-bound. The only dataset
with meaningful remaining downscale is **chestdr (0.742, 85% capped)** → *its* lever is more
resolution / tiling, not preprocessing (border 0.001, binocc 0.959).

### §2.2 CMMD is the standout preprocessing target
Entropy **2.22** bits — lowest of any diagnostic dataset (all other X-ray-family sets 7.3–7.6);
span 0.533; **24.8% of the median frame is blank border, in 100% of images**. Badly-allocated
information both tonally and spatially, at a VLM BA of **0.517 = chance on a binary task**.

stable). ⚠️ **ESTIMATE, not measured** — assumes per-dataset independence (false, §2.3) and is computed
